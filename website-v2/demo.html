Try Karela in your browser.

This is the app, rebuilt for the web. Tap around the way you would on your phone: start a run, report a problem on the street, claim a quest, talk to Ani.

Everything here is demo data. Randel's account, the squad, the guild, the runs and every number are made up. Nothing you do is saved or sent anywhere, and reloading the page starts over.

The demo needs JavaScript to run. Everything else on this site works without it.

Weather in the demo

The map is a drawing, not real streets. Ugac Sur, Ugac Norte and Buntun are real places in Tuguegarao; who holds them here is made up. In the app, Ani is a 3D character and answers anything; here she answers a few set questions.
